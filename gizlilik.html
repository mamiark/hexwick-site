Tek istisna: ödüllü reklam izlemeyi seçerseniz, ödülün doğru kişiye verildiğini doğrulayabilmek için oyuncu kimliğiniz Google'a iletilir. Google bu kimliği yalnız bize geri bildirmek için kullanır. İlerlemeniz, eşyalarınız ve istatistikleriniz gönderilmez — ayrıntısı 3. bölümde.

3) Reklamlar

Oyunda ödüllü reklam var: enerji doldurmak, ücretsiz kostüm kazanmak ya da öldükten sonra kaldığınız yerden devam etmek için isterseniz bir video izlersiniz. Reklamları Google AdMob sağlıyor.

Reklam sistemi siz istemeden çalışmaz. Reklam yazılımı oyun açılışında başlatılmıyor; yalnızca bir reklam düğmesine ilk kez bastığınızda devreye giriyor. Hiç reklam izlemezseniz aşağıdaki veriler hiç toplanmaz.

Bir reklam istediğinizde Google şunları alır:

  • Reklam kimliğiniz — Android'de Google'ın, iOS'ta Apple'ın verdiği, sıfırlayabildiğiniz kimlik
  • Cihaz bilgileri — model, işletim sistemi sürümü, dil, ekran ölçüsü
  • IP adresiniz — buradan yaklaşık konum (şehir/ülke düzeyi) çıkarılabilir
  • Reklam etkileşimleri — reklamın gösterilmesi, izlenmesi, tıklanması
  • Oyuncu kimliğiniz — ödülün doğru kişiye gittiğini doğrulayabilmek için bizim ürettiğimiz kimliği Google'a veriyoruz; Google onu ödül bildiriminde bize geri döndürüyor. Adınız, ilerlemeniz ya da eşyalarınız gitmiyor
  • Tanılama ve performans verisi — çökme kaydı, açılış süresi, donma oranı, enerji kullanımı

Bu verileri biz görmüyoruz ve saklamıyoruz; doğrudan Google'a gider ve Google'ın kendi gizlilik politikasına tabidir: policies.google.com/privacy. Google bu bilgileri reklam seçmek, ölçmek ve sahte tıklamayı engellemek için kullanır.

Reklam kişiselleştirmesini kapatmak

  • iOS: Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → İzleme. Buradan uygulamaların izleme isteğini tamamen kapatabilirsiniz. Oyun ilk reklam isteğinde ayrıca sistemin izin penceresini gösterir; izin vermezseniz reklamlar yine çıkar, sadece ilginize göre seçilmez.
  • Google hesabınızın reklam ayarları: myadcenter.google.com

Kişiselleştirmeyi kapatırsanız reklamlar yine çıkar ama ilginize göre seçilmez. Ödüllerinizde bir değişiklik olmaz.

Privacy Policy — Hexwick

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This page explains what information Hexwick collects, why it is collected, and how you can have it deleted.

1) What we collect

For everyone who opens the game

  • Player name — assigned automatically on first launch (e.g. username0160699284). You may change it. This name appears on the leaderboard.
  • Game progress — unlocked levels, level records
  • In-game currency and items — gold, gems, inventory, equipped gear, costumes, chests
  • Game statistics — monsters and bosses defeated, deaths, rounds played and time played
  • Energy and daily counters
  • Nightmare Mode run times and the gear equipped during those runs
  • A random token we generate to recognise your device

Only if you tap “Link Account”

  • On Android: your Google Play Games player ID
  • On iOS: your Game Center player ID — disabled in this version; no identifier is collected

In both cases the only thing we receive is the player ID issued by that service. It is used solely to find your save on a new device.

We do not collect your email address, your name, your profile photo, your location, or your contacts.

If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google's advertising system uses your advertising ID — see section 3 below.

2) Why we collect it

For two purposes only:

  • So your progress returns when you change phones or reinstall the game
  • So the Nightmare Mode leaderboard works

The game data listed above is not used for ad targeting, profiling, or marketing.

One exception: if you choose to watch a rewarded ad, your player ID is passed to Google so that the reward can be verified as going to the right person. Google uses it only to report back to us. Your progress, items and statistics are not sent — see section 3.

3) Advertising

The game includes rewarded ads: you may choose to watch a video to refill energy, earn a free costume, or continue where you left off after dying. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

The ad system does not run unless you ask for it. The advertising software is not started when the game launches; it initialises only the first time you press an ad button. If you never watch an ad, none of the data below is ever collected.

When you request an ad, Google receives:

  • Your advertising ID — the resettable identifier issued by Google on Android, by Apple on iOS
  • Device information — model, OS version, language, screen size
  • Your IP address — from which approximate location (city/country level) can be derived
  • Ad interactions — whether an ad was shown, watched, or clicked
  • Your player ID — the identifier we generate is passed to Google so the reward can be verified as going to the right person; Google returns it to us in the reward callback. Your name, progress and items are not sent
  • Diagnostic and performance data — crash logs, launch time, hang rate, energy usage

We neither see nor store this data; it goes directly to Google and is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Google uses it to select and measure ads and to prevent fraudulent clicks.

Turning off ad personalisation

You control this from your phone — the game does not see this setting:

  • Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads. There you can reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can turn off tracking requests entirely there. The game also shows the system permission prompt the first time you request an ad; declining still shows ads, they are just not chosen based on your interests.
  • Your Google account ad settings: myadcenter.google.com

Turning off personalisation still shows ads, but they will not be chosen based on your interests. Your rewards are unaffected.

4) We do not share your game data

The game data listed in section 1 (progress, items, statistics, player name) is never sold, and never shared with anyone for marketing purposes.

One exception, rewarded ads: if you choose to watch an ad, your player ID is passed to Google — solely to verify that the reward goes to the right person. Your progress, items, statistics and player name are not sent. See section 3.

Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure (Eastern Europe); Cloudflare acts as a service provider processing data on our behalf. As with any web request, your IP address is visible to Cloudflare; we do not store IP addresses.

In-app purchases are disabled in the iOS version. On Android, payment is handled by Google Play; your card details never reach us. The only thing we receive is the purchase token issued by Play, used solely to verify that the purchase is genuine.

5) Security

All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS.

6) Deleting your account and data

How to request it

  1. Find your player name — it is shown at the top of the Profile screen in the game (e.g. username0160699284).
  2. Email mamyark.1@gmail.com with the subject “Hexwick — account deletion” and include your player name.
  3. We will confirm by email that we received your request.

What is deleted

Everything associated with your Hexwick account:

  • Your player name and account record
  • Your game progress, in-game currency, inventory and costumes
  • Your game statistics, energy and daily counters
  • Your Nightmare Mode run records and leaderboard position
  • The player ID stored when linking your account (Google Play Games / Game Center)
  • The token identifying your device

How long it takes, what is retained

  • Your records are removed from the database within 30 days of your request.
  • Automatic database backup snapshots may retain the data for up to 30 further days, after which they expire automatically. No copies remain after that period.
  • Progress cannot be recovered after deletion.

You can unlink without deleting. In the game, Profile → Unlink removes the account link; it does not delete your data and does not affect the progress stored on your phone.

7) Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
